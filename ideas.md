# Design Brief — Personal Finance Advisor Bot

## Theme Name
Calm Signal Fintech

## Intro
A trustworthy personal finance workspace that makes financial progress feel measurable, calm, and actionable rather than intimidating.

## Design movement
Modern fintech editorial dashboard: clear information hierarchy, quiet surfaces, confident numerals, and small moments of encouraging feedback.

## Core principles
Clarity before decoration; every metric has context; sensitive actions wait for confirmation; empty states always point to a next step; the interface should feel dependable for daily use on desktop and mobile.

## Color philosophy
Deep navy anchors the shell and brand. Teal highlights active navigation and primary actions. Off-white surfaces reduce visual fatigue. Green means positive cash flow or progress, red means negative movement or exceeded budgets, and amber means attention or warnings. Use accessible contrast and avoid gradients.

## Layout paradigm
A desktop sidebar with a compact top bar collapses into a mobile drawer. Dashboard content uses responsive card groups, with the most important financial totals at the top, trend information in the middle, and actionable recommendations below.

## Signature elements
A flat shield with an upward trend cutout represents protection plus progress. Use consistent status chips for Safe, Warning, and Exceeded. Pair large numbers with concise explanatory labels and month context.

## Interaction philosophy
Financial writes wait for server confirmation. AI actions show an inline loading state, then explain whether the result came from a provider or the local fallback. Forms show friendly server validation. Delete actions ask for confirmation. Charts expose plain-text summaries for accessibility.

## Animation
Subtle 150–220ms transitions for navigation, drawers, progress bars, and status changes. No distracting motion or auto-rotating content.

## Typography system
Use a dependable system UI stack. Headings are compact and confident; financial numerals use tabular figures and strong weight. Body copy is concise and supportive.

## Brand essence
Calm, practical, and empowering. The product helps users understand what changed and what they can do next.

## Brand voice
Plainspoken, non-judgmental, and financially responsible. AI content is clearly informational and never framed as regulated financial, tax, or investment advice.

## Wordmark/logo
Text wordmark “Personal Finance Advisor” paired with a simple shield-and-trend symbol. The project icon is an opaque, full-bleed navy square containing a centered teal shield and green upward trend mark with generous clearance.

## Signature brand color
Deep Navy `#102A43`, supported by Teal `#0F766E`, Mint `#34D399`, Amber `#F59E0B`, Coral `#DC5A5A`, and Cloud `#F5F8FB`.
